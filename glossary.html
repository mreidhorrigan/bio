Glossary

The words I had to coin, defined in a sentence or two each. A working list: it grows as the writing does. Walk the slime round the circuit to read them, or read the same words as plain text below.

The words

My own coinages. Terms I only borrowed or extended are not on this list, because somebody else made them.

To (over)illusionate is to sink effort into the illusio, Bourdieu's name for a player's investment in the stakes of a field. The overshoot shows from outside, where acts and speech acts performed with too much belief read as melodramatic, or simply as overacted. Critical theory / metaphysics
Abstractivism is the use of living bodies as symbols. Where extractivism buys the mineral rights under farm fields and Indigenous land and takes the oil, abstractivism visits a community and takes concepts. Its dogma is that making a symbol of each thing is prosocial, though Langer's symbolific drive makes symbol-making about as much a service to the symbolized as eating is. Academia / labour / media
Fanarchaeology is media archaeology done on fan networks and their fanzines, after the anarchaeology Zielinski took from Foucault. It maps the production techniques of zines and the dense lexicons of cyberculture onto the decentralized networks that cyberpunk fiction imagined, which lets cyberpunk be read as vernacular theory. Its methods run from distant reading of digitized zine archives to hacking a mimeograph. SF / horror / infohazard / cyberpunk
Animization is hearing characters in sound, and is distinct from animation. It names a listener's reading of agency and drama into acousmatic material, where a character may be suspected behind sounds that are not voices. It extends Bergsland's vocality, the way sound resembles and so represents the human voice, from the voice to the dramatic character. Voice / sound / music
Antiprofilicity is outsider art's resistance to profilicity, the identity regime Moeller and D'Ambrosio place after sincerity and authenticity, in which a self is managed as a profile. Singing is its type case, because outsider singers perform as though unaware that they have profiles at all. Academia / labour / media
BSGS, the bullshit game simulator, is a proposed genre in Graeber's sense of a bullshit job: it casts the player as a medium for the reproduction of a system whose goals are evil. Descending, Iron Lung, and PVKK all operate machines to no good end. The futility is the player's inability to break the game, which makes the genre a burlesque of meritocracy. Game studies / ludology
Cybernemics is cybernetics practised from inside the system, the study of steering a medium or an institution whose limits register as opaque horizons rather than as black boxes observed from without. Game choices, film-crew hustles, and media piracy are its cases, each a matter of navigating a flow chart from within its own box. Critical theory / metaphysics
Departmentality is the reproduction of a distinct subculture for each department of a motion picture production. Departments gain autonomy by refusing requests and becoming opaque when surveilled, and the macrofield would fail if none of them did. The word torques Foucault's governmentality toward the crew, and what it names is not only a boundary but an anti-solidarity: nonresponse given an ontological structure. Academia / labour / media
Deservance is an object's bond to a way of being treated. It is an association such that the object seems to require its treatment, and it works before norms do, in the symbolific drive Langer described, the attaching of a habit to an object. Deservance is contagious and forms persistent hierarchies, so the power to mark it is protogovernmental. Critical theory / metaphysics
Electrovocology is the study of the electronically mediated voice. It is formed against vocology, Titze and Scherer's science of vocal habilitation, and takes as its object the voice already altered by pitch shifters, vocoders and correction software rather than the voice trained by exercise. Voice / sound / music
The emancipation of the hypercorrect is the freeing of the over-corrected, Auto-Tuned voice, a sequel to Schoenberg's emancipation of the dissonance. To be hypercorrect, in Labov's sense of a class-anxious overshoot, is to over-apply a prestige rule until it is wrong by its own lights. The emancipation is formal rather than political, and the freed voice becomes the new arena of distinction. Voice / sound / music · Z12
Eticapital is capital whose value outside a field differs from its value inside it. The terms come from the etic and emic distinction in anthropology, so eticapital stands against emicapital, its inside counterpart. Peer review is worth a great deal to scholarship within the institution, while the same work is valued outside it by popular approval. Critical theory / metaphysics
Gameshow capitalism is capitalism that centres explicitly ludic frames and extracts value from them as products rather than processes. Its core dynamic is a motion from play to display, in which relations and materials "collapse to a cinematic logic by which events make themselves available for parsing as reductive symbols." It stages C. Thi Nguyen's value capture as a political economy. Game studies / ludology · Z37
Greasy hustle is the tacit, improvised labour that keeps a film set running, the work for which "training manuals do not map hustles." No formal curriculum can specify it, because specifying it would destroy it. It differs from Strauss's articulation work and Vaughan's normalization of deviance in its moral grease, the faintly shameful quality that is exactly why those who know it guard it. Academia / labour / media · Z17
Hauntological security is resignation to a future with unknown but overwhelmingly negative consequences, and a central part of the comfort that small horror games afford. It is Laing and Giddens's ontological security after the future has been cancelled, a home made in the unhomely. The bullshit job simulator soothes rather than terrifies, offering a world whose worst is fully known and bounded. SF / horror / infohazard / cyberpunk · Z14
A holophor is the ideal limit of immersion, a whole sense-world so total that the medium bearing it becomes imperceptible. Where metaphor brings meaning from beyond, a holophor brings the whole, overcoding any sign by which an inhabitant might infer a difference between worlds. Every hyalosign in Deleuze's sense marks a holophor's imperfection. Critical theory / metaphysics
Holophoria is an obsession with immersion, the drive toward a medium so complete that it disappears. A game that achieved it would stop feeling like a game, and insight only ever arrives at an interface, so holophoria defeats itself: perfect immersion would be perfectly boring. Critical theory / metaphysics
Humanishes are the humanish voices of institutional functionaries, announcers, GLaDOS, ghost guides and synthetic assistants, toward which listeners extend graded moral regard. The term follows Cecchetto's humanesis, and its medium is the acousmatic voice, which draws power, as Chion's acousmêtre does, from the gap between frontstage and backstage. The functionary voice is the maintainer made sonic. Voice / sound / music · Z10
Hyalosigning is the activity of producing hyalosigns, Deleuze's crystalline signs of mediation, treated as something media do rather than something films contain. Music, recording, and display all hyalosign when they leave a trace of their own surface, which shifts the question from what a hyalosign is to when mediation becomes perceptible. Critical theory / metaphysics
Infohazardous arts are works that harm by being known. They run on the prophet-punishing, self-fulfilling prophecy, in which learning more is only suffering because the future cannot be changed. The genealogy runs from Lovecraft through cyberpunk ICE to The Ring, and the governing myth is Cassandra, prime myth of the information age, set against Midas, prime myth of capitalism. SF / horror / infohazard / cyberpunk
A ludic no(o)sign is a videogame's sign of withheldness, one that implies thought at work behind an object that will not give everything of itself. The term ports Deleuze's cinematic noosign into games and puns it toward the non-sign: a black box whose continual defiance of measurement makes interpretation close to impossible. also image-cristal vidéoludique. Game studies / ludology
Machine-machine cinema is cinema read as machine code, a mode carrying lectosigns without noosigns. Deleuze's noosign needs an audience inferring a thinking cause, and code passed between machines offers nothing to infer, which is also the condition of a chatbot whose first person is an animation rather than a mind. Critical theory / metaphysics
A margin-worker is a middle manager on a film crew, mediating between departments, whose work absorbs the unglamourous parts of directorship. The job is worked at a space's edges, the doorways and apertures where a production meets what is not itself, enforcing tolerances for other people and absorbing displacement. Because violence haunts margins, the margin-worker goes to the rifts. Academia / labour / media
A mnemosign is a feeling of rememberedness, the sense that something has already been lived. The word is taken back to Mnemosyne, memory herself, whom Deleuze makes the ground of pure memory in Difference and Repetition, rather than to the recollection-image that Cinema 2 calls a mnemosign. Hyalosigns wrought from mnemosigns are the most haunting of all, since déjà vu and the Mandela Effect are mediation showing through in the medium of memory. Critical theory / metaphysics
A no(o)sign is Deleuze's noosign respelled to foreground its negativity: not a sign of thought but a non-sign, a withheld interiority that keeps defying measurement between input and output. A haunted place is a no(o)sign, and so is a film role, defined by the vacancy at its core. Critical theory / metaphysics
Nulltopia is unoccupiable space, the disjunction between a virtual world and the world of whoever attends to it. It works as a limen without feeling like one, being less phenomenal than noumenal, and it turns up in dreams, prosceniums, vehicle windows, screens, and consoles. It converges with Galloway's frustum. Critical theory / metaphysics
An opaque horizon is the inverse of a black box: a system whose walls cannot be seen from inside, and which defeats cybernetic analysis by having too many outputs to measure rather than too few. Immersive media, legitimacy, and subcultural knowledge all present opaque horizons instead of boxes to be modelled from outside. Critical theory / metaphysics
Paideophobia is the limitation and control of children and of play. It relates to the Laius complex, the drive to kill one's children, and shows up wherever play is valued for keeping the young contained. Videogames in the basement are preferred to crimes in the street, which makes games a suburban police. Game studies / ludology
Pathophonies are songs treated as carriers of pathos, sounded records of suffering. The word is formed on pathography, the study of a community with regard to its pathological conditions, and moves that study from writing to sound. A generation's songs sing what the next generation's therapy deals with, which makes them part of a therapeutic cultural cycle without being therapy. also pathosonies. Voice / sound / music
Phobonomics is the economy of fear, the rules by which a culture distributes who gets to enjoy catastrophe and who has to carry it. It prices foreknowledge: the same disaster-knowledge is cheap and pleasurable consumed as spectacle, and costly and implicating borne as responsibility. Catastrophilia for the producer is the Cassandra complex for the grip. SF / horror / infohazard / cyberpunk · Z02
Piratextuality is the condition of media pirated by partial obfuscation, where an illegitimate copy stays legible to viewers while passing unrecognised by a platform's copyright algorithms. Downpitched voices, doubled episodes, and looping piano scores territorialise a text and become its atmosphere. The term extends Grabarczyk's piratext through Bolter and Grusin's hypermediacy. Academia / labour / media
Retropoiesis is the production of the feeling that production has already happened. It is the making of a past, poietic rather than reconstructive, working outward from a conscious moment. It infers the far from the near and imbues knowledge with farness, which makes memory a making rather than a retrieval. Critical theory / metaphysics
Rogue alea is designed chance used to do, legitimately, what petty theft does criminally. Alea is Caillois's name for the play-drive toward randomness, and statistical literacy is far rarer than the literacy in agôn that schools teach. The gap between them enables statistical theft, as when a responsible-gambling campaign presents casinos as passive hosts of randomness and players as agents making decisions. Game studies / ludology
Technopacity is opacity wielded as class power, "an ethic of aggregated mysteries of technologies." Its organizing force is not a set of tools that experts have a tradition of mastering but "the need to resist the understanding of observers." It keeps observers guessing whether its utterances mean anything, since pure noise empties the audience and pure logic would be mapped. It is the political twin of technoscuro. SF / horror / infohazard / cyberpunk · Z45
Technoscuro is the technological chiaroscuro of the dark-Romantic machine world, cyberpunk's play of glare and shadow, screen-glow and gutter-dark. Cyberpunk had names for its content and for its music but not for its light, and technoscuro is distinct from tech-noir, which names a sound rather than a treatment of light. It is hauntology's lighting design. SF / horror / infohazard / cyberpunk · Z09
The game novel is a novel driven by the rules of a game with brutal stakes, framed dystopically so that the game concentrates the storyworld's problems, and ended by an attempt to break the game. It is the twenty-first century's Hollywood novel, running from Connell to The Hunger Games. Where the hero's journey exhausts itself on iteration, a game frame is a narrative engine. Game studies / ludology · Z36
"Theism is animism applied to an absence" is an aphorism of the hyalosign metaphysics, in which animism reads mind into a present thing and theism reads it into a gap. Its companion formula is that theism interprets hyalosigns as noosigns, taking the glassy sign of a medium seen from inside, in Deleuze's cinema-signs, for a sign of thought at work. Critical theory / metaphysics
Trauma capital is the conversion of a place's or a person's wound into exportable image-value, Bourdieu's capital extended to trauma and to the screen image's detachment from its labour. It supplies the production chain that Seltzer's wound culture describes only as demand: who locates the wound, who shoots it, who gentrifies it, who is displaced. The value accrues to the spectator, not the sufferer. also image capital, image-labour. Academia / labour / media · Z25
Truins are haunting sociotechnical facts, truths gone to ruin and ruins that carry truth. The word is made from truth and ruins, and its material is rumour, the hearsay barred from court that is nonetheless a repository of workplace knowledge. Riverview, a partly deinstitutionalized psychiatric hospital and the region's largest film location, is its case. Academia / labour / media
Uncertainty-oriented ontology is a rereading of Harman's object-oriented ontology in which the noumenal is the momentarily unobserved rather than a perpetual unknowable. What looks like an object's withdrawn depth is the part of a pattern not yet transduced into the observer's medium, a noumenon with a mechanism rather than a mystery. Schrödinger's cat is its model, not Lovecraft's unknowable. Critical theory / metaphysics · Z05
Voiceshifting is the practice of electronically modifying a voice's pitch, formants, and thus meanings, to create characters and personae. A capacious term, it gathers the earlier ones together: pitch shifting, formant shifting, pitch correction, vocoding, granulation and varispeed. Scholarship had studied these one tool at a time, above all Auto-Tune, while producers already worked within a continuous space of intended results. Voice / sound / music
x-phonic describes a voice heard on a collapsed euphony and cacophony continuum rather than as one or the other. The x comes from Allan's x-phemism, used where no measure decides whether a phrase is euphemistic or dysphemistic. In the attention economy it marks the voice freed from a fixed bond to one person's timbre, prestigious and unprestigious at once. Voice / sound / music

The penumbra

An antiglossary. I gave my notes to Claude and asked it to write about what the work does not say. These entries are its inventions, not my terms, and any word that turned out to have come from the machine rather than from my drafts is down here too. I keep them so the difference stays visible. They are the shadow the glossary casts.

The metabolization spine has an unwritten immunological complement: some of its moves are autoimmunity, the defence becoming the disease, not digestion. Where digestion incorporates the other, immunity recognizes and refuses it, a third term beside extraction and care. It fails if few harms come from a system's defence rather than its appetite. N01 · zone:penumbra
The platform gamifies music-making exactly as it gamifies play. Repitching to evade censors is a rule-bound, adversarial game with a cancellable referee, and a weird move's value decays once the platform makes it a trackable metric. Voiceshifting is sonic gilded time, and the trending sound is a trophy. N03 · zone:penumbra
Hauntedness is a futures contract, and the rest of the derivatives stack follows. An option on trauma capital is the right, not the obligation, to tell a wound, acquired cheap and exercised only if the narrative appreciates. The option captures the violence exactly, because it transfers risk while reserving upside: the wound bears the risk. N04 · zone:penumbra
Deniable craft is tacit, faintly illegitimate, system-necessary skill that cannot be put in a manual and that the system needs to remain unwritten, because articulating it would both destroy it and expose the deniable violation it performs. Greasy hustle is deniable craft on the set, pirate literacy deniable craft at the codec. N09 · zone:penumbra
Anticipatory grievability is the mournability of what has not yet died but is foreknown to die: a species, a glacier, a neighbourhood, a future, a self. It is differentially distributed too, necropolitics in the future tense, and it has an economy in climate-grief content and the elegiac documentary. N10 · zone:penumbra
Forced transparency is the cell where the subordinate is made transparent in order to be controlled: the surveilled worker, the tracked gig-labourer, the audited welfare recipient. The strong elect their visibility and the weak have theirs chosen for them, which makes chosen opacity a counter-legibility rather than a free-standing aesthetic. N12 · zone:penumbra
The price of foreknowledge is not fixed. It is tuned by media form: the same catastrophic content costs less dread sung cozily, absurdly, or in a child's voice, and more in the menacing downshifted announcer. Voiceshifting is therefore a phobonomic instrument that sets the affective price of an infohazard. N13 · zone:penumbra
The game novel is a Cassandra: a true warning, consumed but disbelieved, read as thrilling entertainment rather than heeded, that comes true. Gamebreaking and shared knowing as cure are one concept, communion breaking an isolating quota, and the realized game's deletion of gamebreaking is recuperation told as the loss of hope. N18 · zone:penumbra
Conspiracy and hyperstition are the same move, both substitutes for a missing cognitive map, differing only in valence. Conspiracy is the paranoid map, a false totality discovered, and hyperstition the performative map, a fiction made real so the totality need only be enacted. A third response maps together, collectively and fallibly. N22 · zone:penumbra
A competitive game always contains irreducible chance, and meritocracy works by relabelling that alea as agôn, presenting a chance-shaped outcome as a merit-earned one. The winner is sacralized as deserving while the eliminated eat the loss as if they had failed. It fails if the disputed outcomes are mostly skill. N43 · zone:penumbra
Attention is the single substance both care and extraction require, so they are zero-sum. Care is the right use of attention, a just and loving gaze directed upon an individual reality, which makes the attention economy an anti-care machine rather than only a thief of time. It fails if heavy capture does not degrade mentalizing. N45 · zone:penumbra
A camera that watches the player's own avatar, and a name for what that does to playing: the player is put in the position of a spectator of themselves. The word came out of the loop passes rather than out of M.'s drafts, and it is already in use elsewhere: Peter de Bolla neologized it in 1995 for the pleasure of seeing while being seen. the penumbra · zone:penumbra
Emersion is the perception of a medium's edge from inside it, the moment an immersive frame becomes noticeable to whoever it encloses. Against a discourse that prizes immersion, it treats the glitch, the mirage, and Deleuze's hyalosign as emersive cues, the only things that make a medium detectable at all. the penumbra · zone:penumbra
Eyecology is the ecology of the image as a means of rule. Its terms run from iconocracy, government by image, through optocracy and sonocracy, forms of rule that operationalize Deleuze's opsigns and sonsigns, to the phobocracy an iconocracy can become. Censorship appears in it as a modern iconoclasm. the penumbra · zone:penumbra
A family of names for laundering: eduwashing, wordwashing and idiotwashing. Each names a way of making something pass by running it through a medium, through a word, or through an affected stupidity. the penumbra · zone:penumbra
