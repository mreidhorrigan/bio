Two kinds of file are here. An engine's program is a physical model published under the GNU General Public License together with the code that is linked with it in its own Worker: you may use, change and pass it on under that licence, and its complete source is in sources/, with the licence's text (sources/GPL-3.0.txt). Everything else – the page, the band of bots and their cores, the studio, each instrument's body and its player's settings – is M. Reid Horrigan's own, exchanges only messages and data with the engines, and is not offered under any open licence: its source is not published.
