Shoggoth proposals

A monster for the walkable world, proposed six ways. C, the iridescent bulk, was adopted and is now the slimeverse's predator, in place of the hunched thing with eyestalks.

The world already has one citizen made of gel: a slime with a single eye, an ink outline and a lit interior. A shoggoth is what that is when there is far too much of it. Lovecraft supplies the constant across all six: a protoplasmic mass that forms and reabsorbs its organs, so eyes open on the surface, hold a moment, and sink back in.

Each design is built from the vocabulary already in the world, so it reads as native rather than imported: gel gradients, a 2px ink silhouette, glowing beads, a slow wobble, and per-instance variation from hash01, so the same shoggoth always looks like itself. The painters take the same (C, b) context buildings.js builds. C now lives in ecology.js as drawPredator, sized for an ecology beast and cut down to suit a dozen of them painting at once; the other five stay here as the roads not taken.
