The slimeverse in three dimensions

Three places on the one 3D engine: the village outdoors, the inside of a house, and the cave under the Glossary. They join up the way they would in the world. Every house lets you in, the Glossary's wellhead and the hatch indoors both lead down, and the rope under the shaft leads back up.

Outdoors

Arrow keys or W and S to walk, A and D to turn, and shift to hurry. Drag to look around, click to walk somewhere, and scroll over the view to pull the camera in or out. The keys belong to the view only while it is on screen.

The three skins

Each skin of the village's isometric world has its 3D version, with the same world, the same things in the same places, and each thing drawn the way that skin draws it. Slimeverse by day has gel dwellings, plants and lakes. Gloomthmaxx is the same world after dark: keep to the light, where the slime's beam, the warm windows and the neon signs are. Bureaucore is a flat paper board of black-edged blocks, with nothing else on it.

What is in each place

  • Outdoors. The village's isometric world, walked in three dimensions: the same world that goes on forever, the plaza and its monument, a house for every place in the village on the same ring and the same roads, the Glossary's wellhead, the same biomes, lakes where the isometric world has water, and its plants on the same ground. Knots of zoogs graze, and shoggoths hunt them on their tentacles.
  • Indoors. Any house, from the inside and at the same size (its door leads back out where you came in): lobes of gel lit from within, a skylight, shelves of books, a table, lamps, a gel bath, a spore-cap in a pot, and a hatch down to the cave. Three zoogs live here. No shoggoth comes in.
  • The cave. The corridor from slime3d.html, shorter and round-edged: the living half with zoogs in it, then the dark half with the shoggoths and the drifting lights.

How it is built

Nothing here is wired into the site. The renderer is engine-3d.js, the same file slime3d.html uses. verse3d.js is what the places share: the slime, the camera, water, the shoggoths, the zoogs, the ways between places, and the plaques. verse3d-scenes.js holds the three places, each a ground, a palette and what stands on it. Only the place on screen runs, and the view stops drawing when it is scrolled away.

Every pool reflects what stands over it, flipped through the water's level. The waves move a reflection as they would on a pond, but never tilt it, and the slime's eye shows in the water only when a mirror would show it. Deep water shows the photo the village's lakes use, under its own colour.

Everything that stands up is solid, the creatures included. Each thing is the size and shape it is in the village's isometric view, measured against one table in slime radii. A building you can walk into is drawn larger than its icon so that it holds a room, and it is the same size from outside and in.
