Signal tower proposals

Signal-tower styles for the two slime skins, each drawn beside the tower the world uses today, in all four states.

A and A-night are now live. The tower used to be the only structure with no per-theme painter: one routine drew the same steel mast in every skin, while houses, growths, kiosks, props, monuments, signposts and ground all changed with the skin. buildings.js now branches on the skin, so the two slime worlds grow over their masts and bureaucore keeps the plain one. The Live row is that shipped painter, loaded from the repository, so it should match A above it. B, C, D, E and F stay here as the alternatives that were not taken.

The states: Every proposal uses one revised vocabulary: unassigned is an unlit fixture rather than today's violet, which is the same violet as the sporecap props in both skins; ready is a steady lamp; playing adds the beat; error blinks, so it does not rely on colour alone. An unlit idle tower also gives a visitor a reason to click it.
