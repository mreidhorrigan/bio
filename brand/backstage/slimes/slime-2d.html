The slime in 2D

The slime as a body rather than a sprite: free, falling, landing, and lying on ground that is not flat.

The site's avatar is a squashing ellipse that always stands on a level tile. This is a proposal for the same creature with a skin and a volume. In a vacuum it is a circle with the eye in the middle, because nothing is pulling on it. Falling, it tapers upward into a slight teardrop. On the ground, its underside takes the shape of what it is lying on while its top stays a dome.

The underside is found by rolling a disc along the ground and tracing the curve its belly makes. That one radius is the slime's surface tension, and it decides everything interesting: a small disc creeps into every crack, a large one spans them, so bridging a gap and draping over a boulder come from the same rule rather than from special cases. The painter is slime-2d.js, standalone and dependency-free.
