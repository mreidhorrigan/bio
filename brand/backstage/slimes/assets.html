World assets

Every drawn thing in the walkable world, off the world and side by side: kiosks, dwellings, alien growths, signal towers, flora, fauna, monuments, signposts, ground, and the palettes. The avatar slimes have their own pages.

These panels run the site's own painters. The page loads theme-technocute.js, theme-technurture.js, theme-technoscure.js, buildings.js and ecology.js from the repository root and gives them a small stub of the engine, so nothing here can fall out of step with the live site. Everything animates on one clock, and honours prefers-reduced-motion.
