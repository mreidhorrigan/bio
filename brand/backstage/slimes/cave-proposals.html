Cave proposals

The whole glossary is underground, and the two halves of it are different caves: the near half holds the coinages, the far half holds what the machine wrote. Three options for that pair, then three for the house in the village that leads down into it.

Every panel is live and on one clock, so the vines sway, the drips fall and the windlass turns. Nothing here is wired into the site: glossary-world.js still draws the world that ships, and the chosen painter moves there afterwards. The creature is the site's own slime-2d.js, so its underside molds to whatever floor each option gives it.

What is being chosen: One option for the pair of caves, and one for the way in. They are independent: any door works with any cave, except that the grown-over lintel assumes the near half has vines in it.
